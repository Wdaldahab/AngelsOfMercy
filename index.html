A little more clarity

Before you take the next step.

Are these live hospital jobs?

The role cards show examples of specialties this platform can support. They are not active job listings. Prepare an inquiry to share your preferences with the recruitment team.

Does registering guarantee me a job?

No. Availability, professional registration, work authorization, interviews, and hospital requirements determine whether a placement is possible.

What if I trained outside the United States?

You can share your education and career preferences. Requirements depend on your target state, credentials, and individual circumstances. Confirm licensure requirements with the relevant state board of nursing. Any work authorization or sponsorship route must be confirmed with the prospective employer and a qualified immigration professional. Nurviant does not promise sponsorship, eligibility, or a placement.

Can hospitals request multiple nurses?

Yes. Select “I’m a hospital” and describe the specialties, number of nurses, location, and timing you need.

Privacy notice — how is my information used?

This page prepares an inquiry in your email app or downloads a copy to your device. It does not save your form entries in browser storage. If you send an email, your email provider and the recipient process it. Online form delivery is not yet verified. Please share only contact details and recruitment preferences; do not include patient data, identity or license documents, or health information. Contact hello@nurviant.com with questions before sharing information. The business operator, retention period, and information-sharing practices must be confirmed before online intake is enabled.
